## Dollar division \ Деление доллара

Two individuals need to decide how to divide a dollar. Person 1 offers person 2 an amount x, and keeps the rest (1 − x). If person 2 accepts this offer, then they respectively receive 1 − x and x. If 2 rejects the offer, then no one receives anything. Each person cares only about the money he/she receives.

## Bikers meeting \ Встреча байкеров

You are riding a bike and in a few seconds you are going to crash into another bike. Your choice is: to go straight, swerve to the left, or swerve to the right. What will you choose?

## Number contest \ Конкурс чисел

Each student is to write his or her name and a number between 0 and 100. The student whose choice is closest to half of the average is the winner.

You and your partner are owners of a firm. Your share is $150, and your partner's share is $100. The tax office informs you that your firm must pay $25. You should determine your share without communicating with your partner. How much will you pay?

## Natural number contest \ Конкурс натуральных чисел

You are playing in a group of people. If each of you wrote the same natural number, your group would win. If each of you wrote different numbers, the one who wrote the maximum number would win, while others would lose.

## Organize a meeting \ Организуйте встречу

You should meet someone in Moscow. Please arrange a meeting, knowing that your partner has received the same instructions.

## Simplified Turing test \ Упрощённый тест Тьюринга

Type a word, proving you are not a computer.

## Lazy evening \ Ленивый вечер

On a scale of 1–10, with 1 being not at all difficult and 10 being extremely difficult, how hard is it for you to choose a movie to watch in the evening?

## Divide the Dollar \ Раздели доллар

Two players simultaneously make demands to divide a dollar. Each player receives his demand if the sum of the demands does not exceed one, a payoff of zero otherwise.

## Mafia \ Мафия

There are some players in the game named Mafia: a civilian, a mafia, a maniac. Who will stay alive the next day?
